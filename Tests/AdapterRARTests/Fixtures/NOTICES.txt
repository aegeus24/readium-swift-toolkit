Original comic-rar5*.cbr and encrypted-rar5.cbr fixtures contain only original colored PNGs and ComicInfo metadata, generated using the official RAR 7.23 macOS command-line tool. They are distributed under the fork BSD 3-Clause license.

Commands: rar a -r -ma5 -s -m3 comic-rar5-solid.cbr . ; rar a -r -ma5 -s- -m3 comic-rar5.cbr . ; rar a -r -ma5 -hpfixture encrypted-rar5.cbr .
The password for the rejection test is fixture.

Unicode RAR4, badcrc, and multipart fixtures originate from mtgto/Unrar.swift at commit 7216007a38ac122dbdeae26cfdaf7f10775c868f, Tests/UnrarTests/fixture.

links-rar4.rar is decoded from libarchive/libarchive libarchive/test/test_read_format_rar_compress_normal.rar.uu and is used only to test rejection of links. Source: https://github.com/libarchive/libarchive

unsafe-path.cbr is a negative test created by replacing a filename in the original RAR5 fixture with ../escape01.png and recalculating the RAR5 header CRC32. It contains no compression implementation.
