# Optional native EPUB page turns

UltraReader's fork adds EPUBNavigatorViewController.pageTurnStyle: slide (the default), curl, and instant. Continuous vertical scrolling and vertical text keep their existing renderer. Curl and instant use committed horizontal swipe recognizers, leaving taps and live HTML selection in place when no turn is running. An active selection prevents swipe navigation.

Curl captures the visible WebKit viewport before and after a successful Readium navigation. A temporary UIPageViewController animates the images over the live navigator. Two-column layouts with at least 560 points use separate left/right surfaces, the native middle spine, and double-sided rendering. Single-column layouts use a minimum spine. The temporary controllers and images are removed after completion, settings changes, resizing, or disappearance. Reduce Motion and instant turns skip the animation. If snapshots are unavailable or invalidated while reflowing, navigation completes without an animation.

This first implementation animates committed swipes and forward/backward buttons. It does not track a curl under the finger or reverse a partially dragged curl. Semantic navigation, layout, text selection, links, TOC, and persisted locators remain owned by Readium. Chapter jumps keep the existing navigation behavior.

Tests/NavigatorTests/EPUB/EPUBPageTurnAnimatorTests.swift covers physical half rendering, failed movement cleanup, and invalidation while waiting for movement. These tests are mirrored in UltraReader's simulator target, which also verifies actual WebKit content, native one/two-leaf controllers, chapter crossings, live HTML after a turn, and font-change invalidation. The upstream scripts/test.sh ReadiumNavigatorTests command was attempted locally but stops because xcbeautify is absent; no upstream-suite success is claimed. Validate visual quality, RTL/vertical/fixed-layout publications, selection gestures, and fold interruptions on varied publications and real devices before release.
